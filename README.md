# 🗡️ Clash of the Wild
**LINK: https://iamdominic2.github.io/Clash-of-The-Wild/**

A Zelda-inspired 2D top-down action RPG. Step into an interactive 2D world armed only with a stone sword: fight off aggressive monsters, dodge enemy projectiles, gain EXP to level up, and manage your inventory with shop items and health potions.


<img width="615" height="460" alt="image (54)" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/0aad89cb-4743-41b0-8881-c824b4dee051" />

## Controls

Use WASD or Arrow Keys to move.
Use H to use a healing potion (critical for boss fights!)
Use Z or Spacebar to attack (**crucial: don't miss where the stone sword is before you venture into the dangerous areas!**

## Shopping
You may stumble upon shops on the way. Choose what to buy carefully, as gold coins are limited in this game!
