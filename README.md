# Clash of The Wild
**LINK: https://iamdominic2.github.io/Clash-of-The-Wild/**

A Zelda-style 2D top-down action RPG. Step into a dangerous 2D world as a hero armed only with a stone sword to ward off aggressive monsters, dodge enemy projectiles, gain EXP to level up, and manage your health with potions and Max HP power-ups!.

## Controls

**(mobile controls are also supported)**
Use WASD or Arrow Keys to move.<BR>
Use H to use a healing potion (critical for boss fights!)<BR>
Use Z or Spacebar to attack (**crucial: don't miss where the stone sword is, otherwise you can't attack!**

## Shopping
You may stumble upon shops on the way. Choose what to buy carefully, as gold coins are limited in this game!

## Boss fights
You can't get out - do your best to kill the big baddie (and its associated mini-minions!).<BR>
Hint: Monsters move directly towards you, so the best way to avoid physical contact is to walk circles around them - however, it is indeed tricky because you need to face them for a split second to unleash your stone sword's might at them!<BR>
